## About PRISM

PRISM (Partnership for Research Into Sentient Machines) is a UK-based registered charity helping to build the field of digital minds. We support research and educational initiatives that prepare society for the challenges posed by AI consciousness, digital minds, and AI moral status. We raise awareness among non-expert audiences, develop talent to help the field grow, and convene the researchers and experts for important conversations.

Field building sits at the heart of our direction. As a young field, digital minds needs infrastructure: ways for newcomers to orient themselves, and spaces that keep researchers and practitioners connected and collaborating. Our programmes span a podcast, the Beginner's Guide to Digital Minds, a quarterly newsletter, a community Slack channel, fellowships, an online course, and expert workshops. A key part of the role is our partnership with Cambridge Digital Minds, with whom we run Fellowships and an Online course. You'll be central to making that collaboration run smoothly and scale well.
