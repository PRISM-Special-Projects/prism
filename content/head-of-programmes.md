## About PRISM

PRISM (Partnership for Research Into Sentient Machines) is a UK-based registered charity helping to build the field of digital minds. We support research and educational initiatives that prepare society for the challenges posed by AI consciousness, digital minds, and AI moral status. We raise awareness among non-expert audiences, develop talent to help the field grow, and convene the researchers and experts for important conversations.

Field building sits at the heart of our direction. As a young field, digital minds need infrastructure: ways for newcomers to orient themselves, and spaces that keep researchers and practitioners connected and collaborating. A key part of the role is our partnership with Cambridge Digital Minds (CDM), with whom we run the Fellowship and the online course. Our collaboration with the University of Cambridge is a major advantage, giving our programmes the reputation, networks, facilities, and convening power to attract exceptional applicants, speakers, and senior researchers.

## Our programmes

- **Digital Minds Fellowship.** An in-person taught programme that identifies and develops promising new talent. We received more than 3,600 applications in 2026 and selected 14 fellows, each matched with an expert mentor.
- **Digital Minds Strategy Workshop.** A structured working meeting that convenes around 50 experts for focused, output-oriented collaboration.
- **Digital Minds Summer Gathering.** Clusters the Fellowship and Strategy Workshop in Cambridge each summer for several weeks.
- **Online Course: Introduction to Digital Minds.** A structured eight-week course for those looking to deepen their knowledge.
- **Digital Minds Newsletter.** Brings new people into the field and keeps the field informed on the latest research and developments.
- **Community infrastructure and new initiatives.** The Digital Minds Community Slack, which supports cross-organisational coordination, and the Beginner's Guide to Digital Minds.

In 2027, we want to grow each of these: a longer, more structured Summer Gathering built around month-long Senior Fellowships, a larger Strategy Workshop, a deeper Fellowship with extensions for the strongest fellows, and more course cohorts.
